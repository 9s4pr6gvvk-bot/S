Making original music can be exhilarating.

It can also be brutal.

It asks a lot of you — creatively, emotionally and psychologically. You are expected to make work that is personal, vulnerable and true, then place it in front of the world and somehow stay steady while people ignore it, misunderstand it, admire it, tear it apart or project onto you.

Add rejection, self-doubt, online pressure, bullying, sexism, racism, homophobia and the strange modern expectation that every artist must also become a content machine, and it is no wonder so many talented people lose confidence, lose direction or lose touch with why they started.

I understand that world.

I'm a singer-songwriter with over ten years of experience fronting a band, alongside years of hands-on work in songwriting, singing, performance, arranging, production, branding, marketing and visual direction.

I know what it is to build something from the ground up. To wear every hat. To keep going when things are difficult. To try to protect the thing that made you want to do this in the first place.

My mentorship programmes are not just about becoming a better singer, songwriter or performer.

They are about becoming stronger, clearer and more resilient as an artist.

Because becoming the best artist you can be is not only about technique. It is also about understanding yourself, trusting your instincts, finding your voice and learning how to stay with yourself when things get hard.

The Mentorship

Mentoring Built Around You

No two artists are the same, so no two programmes are the same either.

We will build your mentorship around your strengths, your weaknesses, your goals and the areas where you need the most support.

You may want to focus on one specific area, or you may need a mixture of support across several.

That might include:

  • Vocal technique and confidence
  • Songwriting and lyrical development
  • Performance and stagecraft
  • Artistic identity and creative direction
  • Branding, visuals and image
  • Marketing and social media strategy
  • Navigating fear, rejection and self-doubt
  • Staying connected to your purpose as an artist

Sometimes what an artist needs is technical help. Sometimes it is creative direction. Sometimes it is emotional support. Often, it is all of the above.

How We Work

How We Work

We begin with a free consultation so I can get a sense of where you are, where you want to go and what kind of support would genuinely help.

From there, we build something around you.

I do not do empty hype or false promises. Anyone guaranteeing to "make you a star" should be treated with the disdain they deserve.

What I do offer is honest guidance, practical tools, real-world experience and support from someone who understands both the craft and the cost of trying to do this properly.

This is for artists who want to grow without losing themselves.

Artists who want to make something real.

Artists who know there is more in them and need the right support to bring it out.
